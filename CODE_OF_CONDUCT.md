# Code of conduct

This is a small open-source project run by one person in their spare time. Keep it pleasant.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient. Many people here are new to Claude Code, to Python, or to markets.
- Assume good faith. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Keep discussions on the project: the method, the code, the record.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks, or publishing anyone's private information.
- Posting stock picks, promoting trading services, or presenting anything in this project as investment advice.
- Sharing trade records or data that are not yours to share.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content and block participants who break these rules. To report a problem privately, use the repository's [security advisory form](https://github.com/Bader-Aloqily/claude-code-stock-picker/security/advisories/new); only the maintainer sees it.
